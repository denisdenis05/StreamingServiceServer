how the pipeline works

three simple steps from album request to local playback

01

scrapes & matches torrents

when an album is queued, background workers query configured music torrent sources, evaluate artist and release similarity, and select the highest quality matching torrent.

[torrent crawler]
Searching: Radiohead - In Rainbows
Match score: 98% • FLAC [24bit/96kHz]
02

downloads & indexes metadata

the pending torrent checker monitors download progress, retrieves album artwork, aligns track numbers, and writes metadata directly into the streaming database.

[metadata engine]
10 Tracks Matched
Cover optimized • Ready for streaming client
03

custom audio file replacement

want a vinyl rip or remastered mix instead? drag & drop audio files onto any album page to immediately swap or update track recordings in your library.
