how the pipeline works

three simple steps from album request to local playback

01

searches soulseek & torrents

when an album is queued, background workers query configured providers in priority order (Soulseek / Torrent), evaluate release completeness & audio quality, and enqueue the best matching source.

[soulseek & torrent crawler]
Searching: Radiohead - In Rainbows
Matched: Soulseek • FLAC [24bit/96kHz]
02

downloads & indexes metadata

the pending download checker monitors provider progress, retrieves album artwork, aligns track numbers, and writes metadata directly into the streaming database.

[metadata engine]
10 Tracks Matched
Cover optimized • Ready for streaming client
03

custom audio file replacement

want a vinyl rip or remastered mix instead? drag & drop audio files onto any album page to immediately swap or update track recordings in your library.

[manual override]
Audio Files Replaced
